## Reader observation protocol

1. Choose prospective buyers or end users fitting the declared segment; record recruiting method and sample size.
2. Present A/B order without claiming that B is better or newer; if possible counterbalance order.
3. Ask open questions before showing any implementation details:
   - Who is this product for?
   - Which concrete problem does it remove?
   - What would change in your work?
   - What do you think the primary button will do?
   - What is unclear or unconvincing?
4. Record direct quotes only with consent and appropriate privacy treatment. Otherwise store anonymized synthesis.
5. Observe the relevant preview/install task separately from page comprehension. Do not coach the solution.
6. Keep **product-truth failures as blockers** even if a variant is more persuasive or attractive.

These are evaluation instructions for a future authorized comparison, **not** evidence that an experiment has happened.
